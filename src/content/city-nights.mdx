## City Nights

A series shot handheld and on a tripod across a few late walks through the city.
The aim was to let the long exposures turn moving traffic and crowds into light
and blur, while the fixed architecture stays sharp.

Shot on a 35mm prime, exposures between one and fifteen seconds.
