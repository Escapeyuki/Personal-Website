## Overview

A cross-sectional momentum strategy on S&P 500 names. Each month we rank names by
their trailing 12-month return (skipping the most recent month), go long the top
decile, and short the bottom decile.

The signal for name $i$ at time $t$:

$$ s_{i,t} = \frac{P_{i,t-1}}{P_{i,t-12}} - 1 $$

## Implementation

The signal is a two-line pandas expression once prices are in a wide frame:

```python
signal = prices.shift(1) / prices.shift(12) - 1
ranks = signal.rank(axis=1, pct=True)
weights = (ranks > 0.9).astype(float) - (ranks < 0.1).astype(float)
```

## Result

The long-short book returned a Sharpe of roughly 0.8 gross of costs over the
backtest window — enough to be interesting, not enough to retire on.
