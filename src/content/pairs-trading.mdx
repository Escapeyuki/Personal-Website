## Overview

Find two names whose prices move together over the long run, trade the spread
when it stretches, and close when it snaps back.

## The spread

We hedge one leg against the other and watch the z-score of the residual spread:

$$ z_t = \frac{s_t - \mu_s}{\sigma_s}, \quad s_t = P^A_t - \beta\,P^B_t $$

Enter when $|z_t| > 2$, exit when it reverts toward $0$.

## Signal

```python
spread = a - beta * b
z = (spread - spread.rolling(60).mean()) / spread.rolling(60).std()
position = -np.sign(z) * (z.abs() > 2)
```

The edge lives entirely in the pair selection — a spurious pair mean-reverts until
it doesn't.
